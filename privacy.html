Privacy

Short version: your message goes to Claude to be rewritten, and Whimsify doesn't keep it.

What gets sent

When you press Whimsify, on the website, in the iPhone app or from the Share button, the app sends your message, the voice you picked, the language and the mode (rewrite or reply) to Whimsify's small server. The server passes them to Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, which writes the three versions and sends them back to you.

What Whimsify keeps

  • Not your messages. Whimsify doesn't store or log the text you write or the versions it gets back.
  • Not your IP address, name or contacts. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • Anonymous usage counts, if you allow them. To see which voices people enjoy, the server records small events such as "a rewrite in Sports commentator, in English" or "level 3 was copied", plus whether it came from the website or the iPhone app. Each event carries a scrambled code that changes every day, so visits can't be linked over time or traced back to you. You can turn this off below at any time.
  • A daily limit counter. To keep the service free, each person gets a limited number of rewrites a day. The counter uses that same scrambled daily code (on iPhone, made from a random ID the app creates for itself) and is deleted after two days.

Claude and Anthropic

Your message is processed by Anthropic to create the rewrite. In the iPhone app, you're asked to agree to this before your first rewrite. How Anthropic handles it is described in Anthropic's privacy policy.

Usage stats

Turning this off stops the website and the iPhone app screen on this device from sending any usage counts. For the Share button, use Privacy at the top of the Whimsify sheet.

On your device

The website and app remember your language choice on your own device. Nothing else about you is saved there.

Children

Whimsify isn't directed at children under 13.
