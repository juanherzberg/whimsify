Support

Something not ringing right? Here's help, and how to reach a real person.

Get in touch

jpf2149@columbia.edu

Questions, bugs, ideas for a new voice: all welcome. Replies usually within a few days.

Whimsify isn't in my Share menu

Select some text first, then tap Share. If Whimsify isn't in the row of apps, scroll to the end, tap More, and add it to your favorites. Step-by-step help.

Some apps don't show Share

A few apps, such as WhatsApp, leave Share out of the typing box. There, copy your message, open Whimsify, paste it in, and copy the version you like back.

"You've used all of today's calls"

To keep Whimsify free, each person gets a set number of rewrites a day. The lines reopen the next day.

"This one's better said plainly"

Whimsify won't rewrite messages that are sexually explicit, threatening, hateful, or meant to bully someone. Romance, flirting and teasing are welcome.

Languages

Whimsify writes back in the language you write in, or in English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Italian, German, Arabic or Japanese if you choose one.

Privacy

Your message is sent to Claude by Anthropic to be rewritten. Whimsify never stores it. Read the privacy policy.
