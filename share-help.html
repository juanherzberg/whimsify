Whimsify from any app

Write your message where you'd normally send it, then let Whimsify give it a better line.

  1. Write and select your message. In Messages, Notes, Mail, WhatsApp or almost any app, type your message, then press on it and choose Select All.
  2. Tap Share. It's in the little menu above the text. If you don't see it, tap the › arrow to find it.
  3. Tap Whimsify in the row of apps.
  4. Dial a voice, then tap the version you like to copy it.
  5. Paste it back over your original message and send.

Don't see Whimsify?

  1. In the Share sheet, scroll the row of apps all the way to the end and tap More.
  2. Tap Edit, then tap the + next to Whimsify to add it to your Favorites.
  3. Drag it to the top so it's always first in line, then tap Done.

Whimsify only shows up when you've selected some text. Your message is rewritten by Claude and isn't stored.
